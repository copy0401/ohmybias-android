0.5.0 — physical keyboard support
• Compose directly on a Bluetooth/USB keyboard: letters compose, digits select candidates, Space commits, Esc clears; Zhuyin/Pinyin modes work too
• Three display modes for physical keyboards (pick in settings, applies immediately): keep the soft keyboard, a floating candidate bubble next to the cursor, or a fixed candidate bar at the bottom of the screen
• Tap Shift to toggle Chinese/English, Shift+letter for temporary English, Shift+Space for full-width space, Shift+8 for the wildcard
• New "keep suggestions with physical keyboard" switch (off by default for touch typing)
• Settings cleanup: bolder section headers, collapsible help and command cheat sheet, version shown at the bottom

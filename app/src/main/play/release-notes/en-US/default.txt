0.5.0 — physical keyboard support
• Compose on a Bluetooth/USB keyboard: letters compose, digits select, Space commits, Esc clears; Zhuyin/Pinyin too
• Three display modes: soft keyboard, floating candidate bubble by the cursor, or a fixed bottom bar
• Tap Shift to toggle Chinese/English; Shift+letter, Shift+Space, Shift+8 for English, full-width space, wildcard
• "Keep suggestions with physical keyboard" switch (off by default)
• Settings: bolder headers, collapsible help, version footer
